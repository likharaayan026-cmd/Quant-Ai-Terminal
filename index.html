Welcome

Welcome to QuantAI Terminal. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information used while providing trading analysis and backtesting features.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, information may include:

  • Trading strategy information entered by you.
  • Selected trading assets and timeframes.
  • Backtesting configuration and simulated trade parameters.
  • Application preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Market data required to provide application functionality.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and improve application features, including strategy analysis, market-data visualization, backtesting calculations and simulated trading reports.

We do not use your trading strategy as a public profile or intentionally publish your strategy information.

Trading & Market Data

The application may use third-party market-data services to obtain cryptocurrency price and candlestick information. Market data is used to calculate and display simulated backtesting results.

Backtesting results are simulations based on historical or available market data and should not be considered guaranteed future results.

AI-Powered Features

Certain features may use artificial intelligence to interpret strategy descriptions, organize trading rules or assist with analysis. Information provided to AI-powered features may be processed by the relevant service provider to perform the requested functionality.

Local Storage

Some application settings, session information and backtesting history may be stored locally on your device using browser or application storage technologies.

Clearing application data or browser storage may remove locally stored information.

The application may interact with third-party services for functionality such as market data, charts, fonts, artificial intelligence processing and other technical services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the policies of services you interact with.

Data Security

The application is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided by a child without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.
